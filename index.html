Healthy Aging & Muscle Wellness

Practical information for staying active, supporting muscle health, and maintaining strength as you get older.

Why Maintaining Muscle Matters as We Age

Maintaining muscle and staying physically active can be an important part of healthy aging. Strength, mobility and recovery all contribute to an active lifestyle.

Building a Healthy Routine

A balanced approach can include regular movement, appropriate resistance exercise, adequate protein and a nutritious diet. Individual needs vary, so it is important to choose an approach that fits your circumstances.

What to Consider Before Choosing a Supplement

Supplements should complement, rather than replace, a balanced diet and healthy lifestyle. Before choosing one, consider its ingredients, intended use, serving instructions and whether it fits your needs.
